A Few New Free Servers Worth Trying

Created2026-11-24|Updated2026-11-26|Tech Talk
|Word Count:358|Reading Time:2mins|Post Views:
TL;DR Oracle's free VPS is hard to grab these days — Altare, Nadaroo, and Fly.io are three new free options: one with full SSH access, one with beefier specs, and one whose free tier just returned.

I wrote earlier about how to apply for an Oracle free VPS, which is still pretty much the best free server out there. The catch is that resources are limited now — unless you upgrade to pay-as-you-go, grabbing an instance is getting really hard.

So I recently went looking for other free server options. As it turns out, I found three, all launched not long ago. Here they are.

1. Altare

URL: https://console.altare.gg/register?ref=04LR2A

This is the only one of the three that offers a full VPS you can reach over SSH. Sign-ups sometimes get rate-limited, so just try again a few times.

You can pick different app types — choose the “Sandbox” type, which gives you a server with SSH access.

Sign in with email, and you’ll need to link a Discord account to start the service. Right now, new registrations mostly only have Stockholm instances available. The free tier gives you 1.25 cores, 4 GB of memory, and 50 GB of storage.

2. Nadaroo

URL: https://free.nodaroo.com/invite/sII5mARGwrTJt_VvCm7Opg

This one has beefier specs than Altare — 2 cores, 6 GB of memory, and 200 GB of storage — but you can only choose from the app types below, so there’s no way to connect over SSH freely.

New instances are also mostly in Stockholm only.

Make sure you register through the invite link above — it gets you 100 credits, which you can spend on servers in popular regions such as cn. I only found out about this after signing up, so I’m stuck with 0 credits now. Register through an invite and you’ll start with 100 free credits.

3. Fly.io

URL: https://fly.io/

This is an established provider — I used it to deploy services years ago. They had a free plan long ago, then dropped it. Yesterday I got an email saying the free plan is back, so I’m sharing it here too.

This one isn’t a VPS either — it’s an environment for deploying backend apps. You can import a service straight from GitHub and have it serving traffic publicly.
